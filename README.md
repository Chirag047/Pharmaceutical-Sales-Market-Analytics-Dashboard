# 💊 Pharmaceutical Sales & Market Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI project** focused on analyzing pharmaceutical sales and market performance. The dashboard visualizes product performance, therapy-area trends, regional sales, and commercial KPIs to support data-driven business analysis.

---

### 📊 Project Features

- 📂 **2 Comprehensive Power BI Dashboards**
- 📌 Built using **SQL**, **Python (Pandas)**, **Excel**, and **Power BI Desktop**
- 📉 Covers **Sales, Product, Therapy Area, and Regional** analytics
- 📌 Designed for business intelligence and life-sciences analytics use cases
- 📊 Analyzes **10,000+ pharmaceutical sales records**

---

### 🧩 Dashboards Overview

#### 💊 1. Sales & Product Dashboard

- Tracks **total revenue, units sold, market share, and growth rate**
- Analyzes performance across **products and therapy areas**
- Highlights top-performing products and revenue contribution
- Provides trend analysis for commercial performance

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **SQL** — Data extraction, joins, filtering, and aggregation
- **Python (Pandas)** — Data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory analysis
- **Microsoft Excel** — Data preparation and validation
- **DAX** — Dynamic measures and calculated metrics

---

### 🧮 SQL & DAX Logic

- SQL queries are used for data extraction, filtering, joins, aggregations, and KPI preparation.
- Python/Pandas is used for data cleaning, transformation, validation, and exploratory analysis.
- DAX measures enable dynamic metrics such as:
  - Total Revenue
  - Units Sold
  - Market Share
  - Growth Rate
  - Average Revenue per Product
  - Revenue by Therapy Area
  - Regional Revenue

---

### 📈 Business Insights Provided

- Identify **top-performing pharmaceutical products**
- Compare revenue across **therapy areas and regions**
- Track **market share and growth trends**
- Analyze product-level commercial performance
- Identify high-performing markets and regional opportunities
- Support data-driven **sales and market analysis**
